# Mirai Bot V3 — Public Mirror

Public copy of Mirai Bot V3 — a Facebook Messenger chatbot with automatic
noprefix, command aliases, and Asia/Dhaka timezone.

## Run

1. `npm install`
2. Put your Facebook cookie in `cookie.txt`
3. Add your UID to `ADMINBOT` in `config.json` (see `config.guide.md`)
4. `npm start`

> Never share your `cookie.txt` — anyone with it can access your Facebook account.
